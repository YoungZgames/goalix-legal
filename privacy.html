2. Information We Collect

GOALIX is primarily designed as an offline/single-player HTML5 game.

The Developer does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from players.

We do not intentionally request or collect:

  • Passwords
  • Government identification numbers
  • Precise physical location
  • Financial account information
  • Health information
  • Private communications

Depending on the platform through which GOALIX is accessed, the platform itself may collect technical or usage information under its own privacy policy.

Information You Enter or Choose in the Game

Some details are typed or chosen by the player inside the Game. They are used only to run the Game on your device:

  • Manager name or alias: an optional name you type for your career save. You do not need to use your real name.
  • Age group: on some platforms the Game asks once for an age group (for example "13–15") only to choose a suitable starting difficulty. It is not asked on platforms that manage age requirements themselves, such as CrazyGames.
  • Language, country and time zone: to choose a display language and to suggest a league from your region, the Game reads your device's language and time-zone settings and, where the platform provides them (for example, the CrazyGames SDK), the language and country of your platform session. This happens on your device. The Game remembers only the display language; it does not store the time zone or country and does not send these settings to the Developer.

This information stays on your device, or in a platform save service as described in Section 4. The Developer does not operate servers that receive it.

4. Local Game Data — HTML5 Storage

GOALIX may store gameplay-related information locally in the player's browser or device.

Depending on the version and browser environment, this may include:

  • Game progress
  • Settings
  • Preferences
  • Unlocks
  • Scores
  • Gameplay statistics
  • In-game progression
  • Other gameplay state

This information may be stored using standard HTML5 browser technologies, including:

  • localStorage
  • IndexedDB
  • Other browser storage mechanisms necessary for game functionality

This locally stored information is generally used to preserve gameplay progress and settings.

Platform save services: When GOALIX is played on a platform that offers its own save service (for example, the CrazyGames data module), game progress and settings may also be saved through that service. If you are logged in to the platform, the platform may keep this copy with your platform account so that you can continue on another device; if you sign in or out while playing, the Game switches to the save of the account that is now active. This copy is processed by the platform under its own privacy policy.

The Developer does not use local browser storage as a means of intentionally collecting sensitive personal information.

Clearing browser data, changing browsers, using private/incognito browsing, or platform-specific storage policies may result in locally stored game progress being lost.
